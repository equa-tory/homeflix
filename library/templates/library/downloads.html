YouTube cookies

Required for downloads. Without cookies YouTube caps quality, often blocks downloads with "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot", and private/unlisted playlists can't be read at all, so downloading is switched off until they're saved. Paste them once — they're stored on the server (not shown again) and used for every download, including ones you start from your phone.

How do I get the cookies?

Do it in a private window so YouTube never rotates the cookies you export (a normal browser session keeps refreshing them, which invalidates the saved copy within days).

Option A — extension (easiest, PC or Firefox for Android)

  1. Install Get cookies.txt LOCALLY (Chrome / Edge) or cookies.txt (Firefox) and allow it in private windows.
  2. Open a private / incognito window, sign in at youtube.com.
  3. In that same tab go to https://www.youtube.com/robots.txt (keep it the only tab open).
  4. Click the extension → Export / Copy the youtube.com cookies.
  5. Close the private window without signing out (signing out invalidates them).
  6. Paste the text in the box above and press Save cookies.

Option B — no extension (PC)

  1. In a private window, sign in at youtube.com, then open https://www.youtube.com/robots.txt.
  2. Press F12 → Network tab → reload the page → click the robots.txt request.
  3. Under Request Headers find cookie:, copy its whole value, and paste it above.
  4. Close the private window without signing out.

From your phone: you only need to do this once, from any device. The cookies live on the server, so afterwards the phone just opens this page and downloads. (Pasting a long cookie string on a phone works fine — copy it from the PC to your phone with any notes/messaging app.)

Cookies expire eventually. If downloads start failing with a sign-in / "not a bot" error, repeat the steps and paste fresh ones.
